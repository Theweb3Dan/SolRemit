# SolRemit

> Fast, secure, and low-cost cross-border remittance on Solana — built for Africa.

## Overview

SolRemit is a decentralized remittance protocol built on the Solana blockchain. It enables instant, low-fee cross-border payments for underserved communities in Africa, starting with Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya. By leveraging Solana's high-speed, low-cost infrastructure, SolRemit eliminates the inefficiencies of traditional remittance services and puts more money in the hands of families who need it most.

---

## The Problem

Every year, the African diaspora sends over $100 billion home to their families. But traditional remittance services make this unnecessarily expensive and slow:

-  Fees of 6–8% per transaction (Western Union, MoneyGram)
-  Transfer delays of 3–5 business days
-  Requires bank accounts that millions of Africans don't have
-  Poor exchange rates that further reduce the amount received

---

## The Solution

SolRemit uses Solana smart contracts to make remittance:

-  **Instant** — transfers settle in under 2 seconds
-  **Cheap** — fees less than $0.01 per transaction
-  **Accessible** — no bank account needed, just a Solana wallet
-  **Global** — send from anywhere, receive anywhere
-  **Secure** — funds are protected by on-chain smart contract logic

---

## Key Features

- **Instant Transfers** — powered by Solana's 65,000 TPS throughput
- **Near-Zero Fees** — fractions of a cent per transaction
- **USDC & SOL Support** — send stablecoins or native SOL
- **On-Chain Verification** — every transaction is transparent and traceable
- **Non-Custodial** — users always control their funds
- **Multi-Country Support** — Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and expanding

---

## How It Works

1. **Sender** connects their Solana wallet and deposits funds (USDC or SOL)
2. **Smart Contract** locks and verifies the funds securely on-chain
3. **Recipient** receives an instant notification and claims funds to their wallet
4. **Transaction** is recorded on the Solana blockchain for full transparency
5. **Both parties** can verify the transfer in real time on Solana Explorer

---

## Smart Contract Security

SolRemit takes security seriously. Our smart contract has been scanned using **VaultMind** — an AI-powered continuous security platform for Solana smart contracts.

Key security measures include:
- Input validation on all instructions
- Ownership checks to prevent unauthorized access
- Protection against reentrancy attacks
- Secure fund locking and release mechanisms
- Regular security scans via VaultMind
